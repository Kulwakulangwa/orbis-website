Eight steps. One standard.

  1. Step 1

    Booking

    📋

    Order confirmed with the client; truck and driver assigned and documents requested.

  2. Step 2

    Loading

    📦

    Truck reports to the load point; loading is confirmed and the load date recorded.

  3. Step 3

    Dispatch

    🚛

    Road manifest issued; truck leaves and the dispatch date is reported to the client.

  4. Step 4

    Pre-clearing

    🛃

    Customs documents and the inward report are sent to the border agent before arrival.

  5. Step 5

    Tracking

    🛰️

    Live GPS and driver check-ins on every leg, with delays reported as they happen.

  6. Step 6

    Border clearing

    🚧

    Status updated at each border until the truck is released and moving again.

  7. Step 7

    Offloading

    🏭

    Arrival at the consignee is confirmed and the offloading date recorded.

  8. Step 8

    Proof of delivery

    ✅

    Signed POD collected and sent to the client, closing the trip.
